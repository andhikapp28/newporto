* **Remediation:** Immediately place a dedicated `Case Studies (E:)` or `Enterprise Projects` icon in the primary desktop icon column (directly below `Work Docs`).

#### Friction Point 3: The Critical ATS PDF Disconnect (P0 Blocker)

  1. Target role states **"IT Web Developer"** (severely downplaying the candidate's Senior System Analyst / Architect caliber).

  3. Pages 2 through 7 contain blank space or embedded certificate scans that cause enterprise ATS parsers (Taleo, Workday, Greenhouse) to drop the application.

* **Issue:** While the operating system chrome and headers offer a bilingual toggle (`EN` &bull; `ID`), all three case study Markdown documents (`approval-workflow-engine.md`, `damri-pumk-system.md`, `damri-push-request-tracker.md`) are written entirely in Bahasa Indonesia. * **Storytelling Gaps & Enhancements:**
  1. **Executive Translation Layer:** The case study currently opens with dense relational schema details. A non-technical recruiter or HR Director risks bouncing before reaching the ROI.
  2. **Structure with the Executive STAR Framework:**
     * **Situation:** PT Nusantara Transindo operates 50+ nationwide branch depots with 3,000+ staff. Capex and procurement approvals took 14–21 days via paper memos and disconnected email threads. Ghost approvals occurred during executive business trips.
     * **Task:** Design and govern an automated enterprise approval engine enforcing BUMN procurement thresholds (Perpres 16/2018), temporary delegation of authority, and tamper-proof audit trails.
     * **Action (The SA + Dev + QA Bridge):**
       - *Analyst:* BPMN 2.0 conditional routing gateways based on document value; formal Pjs acting delegation with date-bounded scope.
       - *Developer:* Optimistic Concurrency Control (`WHERE id = ? AND version = ?`) preventing race conditions during simultaneous branch head sign-offs; RFC 7807 structured error contracts.
       - *QA:* 42-scenario SIT/UAT matrix covering boundary edge cases, circular delegation attempts, and 5-minute database rollback SOP.
     * **Result:** Approval turnaround plummeted from 14 days to <48 hours (85.7% cycle time reduction); 0% double-approval defect rate; 100% compliance during internal BPK/BPKP governance audits.
